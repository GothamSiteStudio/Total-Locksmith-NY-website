Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

1. Who We Are

Total Locksmith NY ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website totallocksmithny.com. We provide 24/7 licensed and insured mobile locksmith services across New York City and Long Island.

Contact us at: totallocksmithny@gmail.com or (929) 925-4024.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when:

  • Submitting a quote or contact form — your name, phone number, email address, borough/location, service type, and message.
  • Calling or emailing us directly — your contact details and service location to dispatch a mobile technician.

We may also collect non-personal, anonymous technical data (such as browser type, device type, and pages visited) automatically when you browse our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your locksmith inquiry or emergency lockout request.
  • Dispatch a mobile locksmith technician to your location and provide service estimates.
  • Honor our five-year service warranty and provide customer support.
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website uses basic functional cookies and Google Fonts for typography. Google may log font requests in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You may disable cookies at any time in your browser settings.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain service records and quote requests for as long as necessary to provide our services, honor warranties, and comply with legal obligations. We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access or misuse.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us at totallocksmithny@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
